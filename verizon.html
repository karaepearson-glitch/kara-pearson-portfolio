Explaining the bill: AI for billing transparency

Customers open their Verizon bill and can't tell why it changed. We designed an AI Bill Summary that explains it in plain language, and 87.5% of testers preferred it.

Project overview

Verizon customers struggle to find billing details, make sense of the numbers, and get past the jargon. Our team of seven partnered with Verizon's Experience Design team to answer one question: how can AI improve the way customers understand their bill?

Over about ten weeks we researched the problem across four carriers, designed an AI-powered bill experience, and tested it with 16 people in a Figma prototype.

Milestone 1

Exploration

We began with 15+ sources of research, a heuristic evaluation against Nielsen's 10 heuristics, review mining across Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, and Mint Mobile, and 7 customer interviews. The evaluation showed that Verizon gives customers almost no visibility into why their bill changes.

Customers feel "surprised" by their bills, and contacting support feels like a hassle.

Our interviews pointed to the same fix: customers want proactive automation and summaries they can trust.

Image: research synthesis / interview findings

Milestone 2

Research

We compared six apps (T-Mobile, AT&T, Rocket Money, Xfinity, Mint Mobile, and Google Fi) and ran five cognitive walkthroughs to see where billing breaks down. Two personas anchored our decisions: Sarah, 42, an HR consultant managing a family plan at $265–310 a month, and Jason, 26, a marketing associate paying $85–110 a month.

Journey mapping showed three problems to solve: billing details are hard to find, the numbers are hard to understand, and the explanations are full of jargon.

Image: personas and journey map

Milestone 3

Design & testing

Each of us sketched alone, we merged the strongest ideas on a whiteboard, and then built mid-fidelity screens in Figma. The solution has four parts:

  • AI Bill Summary: a personalized, plain-language explanation of why the bill changed and who or what caused it.
  • Bill Breakdown: a monthly trend chart, a per-member breakdown, and an FAQ that answers questions like "How can I lower my bill?"
  • Bill PDF with AI: highlighted line items you can click, plus an "Ask me anything about your bill" input.
  • Home shortcut: a balance snapshot with one tap into the breakdown and the AI.
Image: sketches and whiteboard consolidation
Image: Bill Breakdown and AI Bill Summary screens

We tested five screens with 16 participants, using A/B tests and 20–30 minute think-aloud walkthroughs.

Results

87.5% of participants (14 of 16) were satisfied, beating our 80% goal.

The AI Bill Summary significantly improved how well people understood their bill, and testers consistently preferred simpler, more visual layouts. Navigation clarity and task flow are the next problems to solve.
