About

I turn user insight into products people actually enjoy using, and I lead teams to get there faster.

I'm a UX designer, researcher, and project manager. I graduated from Purdue in May 2026 with a B.S. in User Experience Design, a minor in Psychology, and a certificate in Collaborative Leadership.

Now I'm a Product Designer at Microsoft, where I interned the summer before. I design enterprise workflows alongside PMs and engineers, and I'm learning product management and design side by side.

I got into UX by coaching Special Olympics and editing a magazine. Both taught me to listen first, communicate clearly, and design for people with very different needs.

Today I pair that with AI. It speeds up my planning, research, and team coordination, so more of our time goes to the people we're designing for.

Away from the screen, I'm racing across sand dunes or exploring somewhere new.
